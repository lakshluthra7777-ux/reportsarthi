About ReportSaathi

Millions of people in India get blood tests every year but find the reports hard to read: dozens of values, medical terms and ranges, and often no time with the doctor to ask questions. ReportSaathi is a final year B.Tech project that uses AI to read a lab report (PDF or photo), flag values outside the normal range, explain them in simple English, Hindi or Hinglish, and track a family's results over time.

Built with

Python Flask SQLite Gemini API (vision + PDF) Structured outputs Chart.js

Important

ReportSaathi helps you understand reports. It does not diagnose illness or suggest medicines. Always discuss your results with a qualified doctor.
